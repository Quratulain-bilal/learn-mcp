# Pakistan's First FDE Hackathon: Build and Sell MCP Apps 🇵🇰

**Prove your skills. Earn your place on a team. Build AI products for the world.**

Zia Khan and his team are forming teams of Pakistan's most talented AI developers to build **Business MCP Apps for the international market**. This hackathon is how you earn a place on one of them.

👤 About Zia Khan: [linkedin.com/in/ziaukhan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ziaukhan/)

**What is an FDE?** A Forward Deployed Engineer doesn't just write code. They understand business problems, build AI solutions and deploy them for real customers. The same skills make a startup founder.

## Who can take part

- Open to everyone in Pakistan with basic programming knowledge. You do **not** need to be a student of any program.
- Entry is completely free. You use your own AI subscription and cloud accounts. A paid Claude plan is recommended, and the repo's cost guide ([Affordable Agentic AI Development](affordable-agentic-ai.md), with [Costs and cleanup](costs-and-cleanup.md)) shows how to keep spending low.

## The challenge

Complete **[Build and Sell MCP Apps: The CCDV-F Bootcamp Part I](../README.md)**, the hands-on companion to **[The AI Agent Factory](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/)** book.

- ✅ Build a real, remote MCP server for Stockroom, a practice client, across 21 hands-on steps
- ✅ Add secure sign-in, a production database and multi-tenancy
- ✅ Ship interactive MCP Apps that work inside Claude and ChatGPT
- ✅ Finish with a tested, deployed MCP app and a commercial proposal

You write the code yourself, with Claude as your coding partner. There is no solution code to copy. Your fork is your proof.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is a serious challenge.** The full bootcamp is over 125 hours of work, so plan your time. Quality matters more than quantity: an outstanding partial submission beats a rushed complete one, and may also be selected.

## The reward 🌍

- 🏅 Selected participants will work alongside **Zia Khan and his team**, combining business ideas, technical expertise and mentorship to develop AI products for international customers.
- 🏅 Selection is on merit: working features, security, how well you understand and can defend your own code, and business value. Taking part does not guarantee a place.
- 🏅 Selected participants will be contacted after judging, and the terms of working together will be shared before any work begins.
