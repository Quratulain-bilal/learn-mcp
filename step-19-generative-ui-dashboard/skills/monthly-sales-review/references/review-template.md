# Review template

Fill in from the tool results. Keep the headings; drop a section only if there's truly nothing to say.

```
## <Month YYYY> sales review

**Revenue: <amount> (<+/-amount>, <+/-%> vs <previous month>)** from <orders> orders.

**What drove it**
- Top product: <name>, <amount> (<share>% of the month).
- Top customer: <name> (<city>), <amount> (<share>%).
- <Anything at or above the company's threshold, or a notable drop.>

**Recommendations**
1. <Specific action tied to a number above.>
2. <Second action, if the numbers support one.>

<One line on anything the data can't tell us, e.g. "We can't see stock levels from here.">
```

Example of a good recommendation: "Standing Desk sales halved from March to April (8 to 4 units); ask the two biggest buyers whether they've paused office fit-outs."

Example of a bad one: "Consider running a promotion to boost sales." (not tied to anything shown)
