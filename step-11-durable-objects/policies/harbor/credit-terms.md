# Credit terms (Harbor Office Supply)

1. Business customers with a signed credit application may pay invoices within 30 days (net 30).
2. The standard credit limit for a new business account is 5,000 USD.
3. Credit limits above 20,000 USD need approval from the finance director.
4. Invoices more than 15 days overdue put the account on hold until they are paid.
5. Customers who pay within 10 days of the invoice date receive a 2% early-payment discount.
