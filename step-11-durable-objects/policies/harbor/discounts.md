# Discount policy (Harbor Office Supply)

1. Sales staff may give a discount of up to 15% on any single order without approval.
2. Discounts above 15% and up to 25% need written approval from the sales manager before the order is confirmed.
3. New customers may receive a one-time welcome discount of 10% on their first order.
4. Discounts never apply to clearance items, gift cards or delivery charges.
5. Discounts cannot be combined: if a customer qualifies for two, apply the larger one.
6. Orders of 50 or more units of one product qualify for a volume discount of 12%.
