# Returns policy (Harbor Office Supply)

1. Customers may return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
2. Opened items may be returned within 14 days if they are in resaleable condition; a 10% restocking fee applies.
3. Furniture that has been assembled cannot be returned unless it is faulty.
4. Faulty items are replaced or refunded at the customer's choice within 12 months of delivery.
5. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return.
