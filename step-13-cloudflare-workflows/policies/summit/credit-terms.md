# Credit terms (Summit Workspace)

1. Approved business customers may pay invoices within 45 days (net 45).
2. The standard credit limit for a new business account is 10,000 USD.
3. A 1.5% monthly late fee is charged on invoices that are past due.
4. Accounts with invoices more than 30 days overdue are moved to prepayment only.
5. Credit applications are reviewed by the finance team within 3 business days.
