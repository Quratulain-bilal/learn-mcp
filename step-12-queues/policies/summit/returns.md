# Returns policy (Summit Workspace)

1. Unopened items may be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund.
2. Opened items can only be exchanged for store credit, within 30 days of delivery.
3. Custom-made or engraved items cannot be returned.
4. The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or was sent in error.
5. Damaged deliveries must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.
