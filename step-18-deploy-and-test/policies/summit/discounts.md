# Discount policy (Summit Workspace)

1. Sales staff may give a discount of up to 10% on any single order without approval.
2. Any discount above 10% needs approval from the regional director, and discounts above 20% are never allowed.
3. New customers do not receive a welcome discount; offer free delivery on the first order instead.
4. Discounts never apply to delivery charges or installation services.
5. Public sector customers receive a fixed 8% discount on every order.
